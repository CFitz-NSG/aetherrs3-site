How to vote and claim

01 · Find your link

Use your character

Type ;;vote in game for your character-linked MoparScape vote URL. The public portal above is also available for browsing our listing.

02 · Complete the vote

Follow the provider

Use the voting site's own confirmation flow. A visit or button click on this page does not count as a confirmed vote.

03 · Claim in game

Leave two free slots

Return to a safe area and use ;;claimvote. The world verifies the provider receipt before issuing its reward.

Check that you used the character-linked URL and finished the provider's confirmation. Leave two inventory spaces and retry the claim in a safe area. If the world says verification is unavailable, include the exact message in your support report.
