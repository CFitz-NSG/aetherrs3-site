November 6, 2026Quality of lifeCompanions

Bank, pet and quality-of-life fixes

Bank tabs keep their organization and the game handles ordinary respawns more consistently.

  • Click or drag to deposit backpack items into the numbered bank tab you selected. Item moves between tabs survive reopening the bank.
  • A called cosmetic pet follows after an ordinary respawn. Both item and cosmetic pets let NPCs pass.
  • Supported Slayer tasks show a teleport button when there is a destination. Bring an enchanted gem.
  • Use action bar food by click or keybind. Reopen Area Loot after a death without reconnecting.

November 6, 2026EventsQuality of life

World events and everyday improvements

Weekend Double XP and an hourly rotation give you different reasons to log in throughout the week.

  • Check the current bonus with ;;events. Hourly events can overlap the weekend bonus.
  • Move Area Loot, group matching drops and keep the panel open in combat. Use filters, a block list or Space to collect all.
  • Hold Shift and click a backpack item to drop it. Ctrl+S opens Companions.
  • Game mode, donor and staff badges can appear together in chat. New players receive starter guidance and equipment.
  • Harmony Home restores your resources. The Summoning supplier carries blank pouches, spirit shards, elder energy and recipe ingredients.

November 5, 2026Getting startedQuality of life

Ironman starter kit and supplies

Ironman and Hardcore Ironman can reclaim missing starter equipment with ;;ironkit.

  • The kit has recoloured hybrid equipment you can use at level 1, with adamant-tier stats.
  • The bow needs no ammunition. While equipped, the staff covers runes for eligible combat and utility spells through level 55.
  • Rook at Home sells basic equipment and supplies for coins to eligible accounts. The Home stall can repeat up to ten theft attempts.

November 5, 2026CompanionsProgression

Companion progression

Train individual companions, choose their talents and build toward prestige and Ascension.

  • Open Companions with Ctrl+S. The tree shows prerequisites, current effects, point costs and upgrade availability.
  • The active follower earns eligible companion XP. Foundation, Mastery and individual signatures offer different ways to train.
  • After prestiging, choose an Ascension path with automatic effects. Review its conditions and reset costs in the journal.
  • An early disconnect during world entry now preserves a saved familiar that has yet to finish restoring.

November 3, 2026BossesQuality of life

Boss entry and encounter feedback

Rise of the Six and solo Vorago are easier to enter. Araxxor has clearer mechanics and saved loot controls.

  • Search for Rise of the Six in the Atlas. Well and portal entry are free and keep your owned totems.
  • Solo Vorago entry no longer requires or removes life points. Approach his head from the north side of the lobby.
  • Araxxor’s cocoon, web, light and acid ramp have clearer visual feedback. Phase health and spider death animations are restored.
  • Open the saved loot window to choose Take or Bank.

October 29, 2026ProgressionQuality of life

Reward reliability, Rush and Prifddinas

Earned supplies and rewards survive more of the failure cases that used to interrupt a claim.

  • Daily hand-ins protect supplies and earned tasks when XP cannot be awarded. Mystery bag claims keep their saved roll through a retry.
  • Reaper Slayer XP stays pending until the XP award succeeds. Hydrix jewellery keeps the charges of the actual copy you wear or bank.
  • Rush of Blood gained supported practice groups and better chest delivery. Prifddinas gained more supported memoriam interactions and earned-copy recovery.

October 27, 2026QuestsQuality of life

Explorer, Slayer and world restoration

Atlas, Commands and Journal share a larger Explorer design with search, clearer navigation and movable panels.

  • Atlas has destination picture cards. Commands has readable command rows and Copy. Journal shows recorded starter progress and guidance.
  • Slayer and Reaper services use their reward interfaces. Supported Co-op rewards depend on recorded paired contributions.
  • Smoking Kills runs through the tomb, well and Banshee Mistress finale. Back to my Roots gained its earned eastern patch fight, quest finale and seed.

October 25, 2026QuestsBosses

Quests, Dominion Tower and boss restoration

Five additional quests gained earned endings, with new practice and boss routes across the world.

  • Finish Shilo Village, Rune Mysteries, One Small Favour, Haunted Mine and Spirits of the Elid through their quest actions and final hand-ins.
  • Dominion Tower added playable ordinary encounters, Specials, group Rumble and an earned roof. Combat level 110 is the supported entry requirement.
  • Custom Karmathulu and the modern Giant Mole chambers joined the boss roster. Vorago and Araxxor gained additional encounter and earned loot handling.

October 16, 2026QuestsBosses

Fairy Tale I and distinct boss rewards

Fairy Tale I gained its playable enchantment, Tanglefoot fight and earned finale.

  • Collect the Queen’s secateurs and hand them in to complete the quest. The enchantment uses the ingredients named in your journal.
  • Max and Lucien each gained their own reward table. Oracle retained its existing custom table.
  • The native XP lamp skill grid works with Necromancy. Cave eel, Mort Myre eel and cave frogspawn fishing have supported native interactions.

October 15, 2026Getting startedProgression

Veilmarket, referrals and combat cooldowns

The Veilmarket discovery route and its traders gained restored native interactions.

  • Speak to Vesper and earn Veilmarket access. The normal travel route works once discovered.
  • Eligible human accounts can use ;;ref NSG for the one-time referral bundle after character setup. Leave room for all the rewards.
  • Combat and Necromancy cooldowns retain timers and shared groups. The Challenges tab gained more supported daily activity and hand-in routes.
