Play a track

Open the in-game Music Player, turn up Music in the audio settings and select an available track. Sound effects and music have separate controls.

Troubleshoot

When music is silent

Try a custom track and a normal game track, then check the Windows volume mixer for the game window. Launch through the current updater if only custom tracks are silent.

Suggest a track

Paste a YouTube video link and prepare a suggestion to post in the AetherRS3 Discord. The website leaves the message in your hands. Suggestions are reviewed before anything is added to the game.
